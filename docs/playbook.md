# Play Factorio through the MCP tools

Check `factorio_status`, observe the world, and identify the user's save before spending items. Use the window the user provided. When a session is interrupted by a change of focus, wait until the operator is ready and observe again. Never open the Lua console, change game settings, install mods, restart a world or overwrite a different save.

For the first live check, open inventory with `e` and observe. Click an empty slot or a harmless tab and verify an on-screen response. Verify one world interaction with a mineable rock or other useful starting resource. Stop for diagnosis if a click receipt succeeds and the game shows no effect.

For a fresh Nauvis start, inspect the closest iron, coal, copper, stone and water in map view. Record where the patches are relative to spawn. Use the starting burner drill and furnace for direct-feed iron, then use those plates to make more drills and a fuel supply. Collect fuel from the closest accessible source and move it in batches. Open the inventory or relevant machine GUI to verify that mined items or smelted plates actually arrived.

Prioritize coal, iron and copper throughput, steam power, and a powered lab. Start research from a small hand-crafted batch of red science and move the repeatable recipes to assemblers and belts as soon as those machines are available. Use production time to prepare the next bottleneck. Avoid idle waits when there is useful construction or scouting to do. Check existing inventory and chest contents before crafting duplicates.

If the objective is a robot network, progress through green and chemical science to the robotics technologies. Build powered roboports in the main factory so their orange logistics areas touch. Give construction robots access to repair packs and commonly used parts, and verify a small construction job before expanding. Keep remote outposts separate when connecting them would send robots through unsafe territory.

When securing the base, inspect map and pollution coverage and prioritize nests threatening the factory and its supplies. Use fielded equipment and stocked ammo and observe after each engagement. With artillery available, let covered outposts handle recurring expansion. Keep reports grounded in visible outcomes such as products finished, power supplied, logistics coverage and confirmed destroyed nests.

Batch inputs only when the first action leaves the rest predictable. Re-observe after moving, changing zoom, opening a new GUI, or any ambiguous outcome. Save completed milestones to the intended save using the normal game UI.
